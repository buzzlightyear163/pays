How it pays

One round.
One stroke.

Every time a Holder Rewards coin pays its holders, that payout round becomes one engraved stroke. Where it starts is when it paid. How long it is, and how dark, is what it paid per $1k held.

Coins that really pay fill their ring. Coins that only carry the label leave it almost bare.

01

Every trade pays a fee.

On a Holder Rewards coin, the creator fee on each trade is set aside for the coin's holders.

02

pump.fun pays it out in rounds.

Through the day, each round sends those fees to every wallet holding more than $20 of the coin, split by how much it holds.

03

PAYS reads every round.

Each round is a public on-chain payout event: the amount and the wallets it reached. PAYS reads them all.
